Lasagna

Description

This comforting lasagna layers tender pasta with a rich tomato and beef sauce, creamy ricotta, and mozzarella. It bakes until bubbling and golden on top.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 450 g ground beef
  • 700 ml tomato pasta sauce
  • 425 g ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Cook the noodles according to the package, then drain.
  2. Brown the beef in a skillet. Stir in the tomato sauce, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Mix the ricotta with the egg and half of the Parmesan.
  4. Spread a little meat sauce in a baking dish. Layer noodles, ricotta, meat sauce, and mozzarella; repeat, finishing with sauce and remaining cheeses.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Uncover and bake 15 minutes more until golden and bubbling. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
