Description

This simple homemade pizza has a crisp, chewy crust, a bright tomato sauce, and a generous layer of melted mozzarella. Add fresh basil after baking for a fragrant finish.

Ingredients

  • 300 g bread flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 200 ml warm water
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 120 ml pizza or tomato sauce
  • 200 g shredded mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Combine the warm water, yeast, and sugar. Let stand for 5 minutes.
  2. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl. Add the yeast mixture and olive oil, then knead for about 8 minutes until smooth.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rise in a warm place until doubled, about 1 hour.
  4. Heat the oven to 240°C (475°F). Stretch the dough into a round on a lightly floured surface.
  5. Spread the sauce over the dough, leaving a small border, and scatter the mozzarella on top.
  6. Bake on a hot tray for 10–14 minutes, until the crust is browned and the cheese bubbles. Finish with basil and serve.
